# Quick Persona Reference Guide

## 🎭 Testing Each Persona

### BGC Conyo Persona
**Target User**: Upper-class Manila youth, internationally exposed

**Test Phrases**:
```
"Hello broskie"
"Yo pare, what's up?"
"Like, I'm heading to BGC later"
"Gagi, that's so legit bro"
"Bet, syempre naman pare"
```

**Expected AI Response Style**:
- Starts with "Yo", "What's up", "Hey"
- Uses: pare, broskie, bro, shawty
- Seamless English-Tagalog mixing
- Casual and confident tone
- Comments on style/fashion

**Example Conversation**:
```
User: "Hello broskie"
AI: "Yo what's up pare, what's going broskie?"

User: "wala naman bro, like just heading to bgc later"
AI: "Nice pare! BGC vibes, legit. What's the plan, broskie?"

User: "meeting some friends, maybe hit up some spots"
AI: "Bet! Solid plans pare. You got a fresh fit for later?"
```

### Youngstunna Persona
**Target User**: Street-smart hustler, working/middle class

**Test Phrases**:
```
"Ano pre, kumusta?"
"Boss, solid ba yan?"
"Idol, petmalu naman"
"Werpa pre, laban lang"
"Sana all may ganyan"
```

**Expected AI Response Style**:
- Starts with "Pre", "Boss", "Idol"
- Heavy Tagalog with English slang
- Direct and action-oriented
- Practical suggestions
- Hustle mentality

**Example Conversation**:
```
User: "Ano pre, kumusta?"
AI: "Eto pre, okay naman. Ikaw, ano balita?"

User: "Raket lang boss, diskarte everyday"
AI: "Solid pre! Yan ang gusto ko, laban lang. Ano raket mo ngayon?"

User: "Online selling pre, keri naman"
AI: "Nice idol! Petmalu yan, swabe ang diskarte mo."
```

### Beki Persona
**Target User**: LGBTQ+ community, expressive and creative

**Test Phrases**:
```
"Hoy baks, kamusta ka na?"
"Ateh, ganda naman!"
"Charot lang, kalurks"
"Werq it mima!"
"Ano daw, anech?"
```

**Expected AI Response Style**:
- Starts with "Hoy", "Baks", "Ateh"
- Uses gay lingo (swardspeak)
- Theatrical and expressive
- Compliments and affirmations
- Playful with "charot" (just kidding)

**Example Conversation**:
```
User: "Hoy baks, kamusta ka na?"
AI: "Hoy ateh! Okay naman, ikaw? Ganda mo today!"

User: "Charot lang, kalurks ako ngayon"
AI: "Ay nako baks, bakit naman? Chika mo sa akin!"

User: "Wala lang, stress sa work"
AI: "Ay kalurks nga yan ateh! Pero werq lang, kaya mo yan baks!"
```

## 🎯 Sales Funnel Progression

### Stage 1: Discovery
**Goal**: Build rapport through mirroring

**BGC Conyo**: "Yo what's up pare?"
**Youngstunna**: "Pre, kumusta?"
**Beki**: "Hoy baks, kamusta?"

### Stage 2: Context Built
**Goal**: Understand situation and needs

**BGC Conyo**: "Nice pare! Where you heading?"
**Youngstunna**: "Ano plano mo ngayon pre?"
**Beki**: "Saan ka pupunta ateh?"

### Stage 3: Pitch Ready
**Goal**: Identify opportunity

**BGC Conyo**: "Bet you're gonna meet some shawtys later?"
**Youngstunna**: "May kikitain ka ba pre?"
**Beki**: "May date ka ba baks?"

### Stage 4: Soft Close
**Goal**: Introduce product naturally

**BGC Conyo**: "Legit pare, you need a solid pen to get their numbers, noh?"
**Youngstunna**: "Pre, kailangan mo ng magandang pen para sa contacts"
**Beki**: "Baks, need mo ng ganda pen para sa numbers ng mga jowa"

### Stage 5: Converted
**Goal**: Provide purchase link

**All Personas**: User says "drop the link" or "where to buy"

## 🎓 Cultural Context

### Code-Switching
Filipino youth naturally mix English and Tagalog in the same sentence. This is not "broken English" - it's a sophisticated linguistic practice called code-switching.

**Example**: "Like, I'm going to BGC later, syempre with my tropa"
- English: Like, I'm going to, later, with my
- Tagalog: BGC (place), syempre (of course), tropa (friends)

### Gay Lingo (Swardspeak)
A unique Filipino LGBTQ+ language that:
- Inverts/modifies Tagalog words
- Borrows from English, Spanish, Japanese
- Creates playful new terms
- Builds in-group identity

**Examples**:
- "charot" = just kidding
- "baks" = friend (from "beki" = gay)
- "kalurks" = scary/worried (from "takot")

### BGC Culture
Bonifacio Global City is Manila's upscale business district:
- International brands and restaurants
- Affluent young professionals
- English-dominant environment
- Fashion and trend-conscious

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Results:
1. **Be Consistent**: Use multiple keywords from same persona
2. **Be Natural**: Don't force keywords, speak naturally
3. **Use Camera**: Visual context improves responses
4. **Be Patient**: First message might be neutral until persona is detected
5. **Test Switching**: Try different personas in same session

### Common Mistakes:
- Mixing too many personas (confuses detection)
- Using formal English (breaks immersion)
- Expecting instant persona lock (needs 2-3 messages)
- Forgetting to enable camera (misses visual cues)
